## Feedback-led product actions

The current release addresses the recurring usability themes with:

- Beginner onboarding tabs for **Start here**, **Field guide**, and **Signing & safety**.
- Role-guided Donor, Coordinator, Merchant, and Verifier routes that clarify the next action without pretending to grant authority.
- A deterministic clean/disputed walkthrough before a user signs a Testnet transaction.
- Explicit pending, empty, success, rejection, and recoverable-error states.
- Mobile responsive/accessibility checks and visual baselines for every Aid role route.
- Privacy guidance that limits public/on-chain inputs to opaque IDs and fixed-width digests.
